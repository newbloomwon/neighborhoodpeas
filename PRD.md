# NYC Map Agent: walk your city before you live in it

## The big idea
NYC Map Agent is a voice-first AI city companion that lets anyone walk the five boroughs block by block, live, without leaving the couch. Not a dashboard or a heatmap you squint at: a guided, AI-narrated, street-level walk through the data the city already has. Every block tells you its story: how loud it is, how safe it is, and whether the city has actually been answering.

## The moat: accessibility you can feel
Every civic data tool is built for analysts. This one is built for everyone. A blind grandmother, a Spanish-speaking dad, and a teenager on a phone all get the same superpower: say where you are and start walking. Zero learning curve and zero clicks required. Accessibility is the core of the product, not a box we check at the end.

## The magic moment
"Walk me from my place to my kid's school."

The camera drops to street level. A calm voice starts guiding: "You're on Knickerbocker Avenue. This block had 41 noise complaints last month, mostly late-night parties. Twelve are still open. Next block, crossing Myrtle: 3 crashes this year, one involving a pedestrian. Heads up at this corner." The 3D city rises around you as you move. It feels like a walk with a friend who knows everything.

## Who it's for
- Parents scouting a safe route to school
- Renters and buyers test-driving a block before they sign
- Neighbors and community boards who need receipts, not rumors
- Blind, low-vision, older, and non-English-speaking New Yorkers, who have been locked out of civic data until now

## Killer features
1. **Walk Mode (the headline feature).** A first-person, street-by-street, block-by-block walk on a 3D map. You move with your voice ("next block," "turn left on Myrtle"), with arrow keys, or by tapping. Every block is narrated in real time.
2. **Talk to your city.** A conversational AI with push-to-talk voice in English and Spanish. It's grounded in real data, so it never makes up a number.
3. **Accountability Lens.** For any block, see how long the city took to respond, how many complaints were closed with no action, and how many keep coming back. One tap exports a shareable report for your council member.
4. **Safe Route Scout.** Plan a walk and get a crash risk readout for every corner along the way, with pedestrian and cyclist injuries flagged.
5. **Glass Box AI.** A live panel shows the agents working in real time: what they searched, what they found, and where every number came from. Transparent, trustworthy, and explainable.
6. **Accessible by default.** Fully usable with voice only or keyboard only, with captions always on, high contrast, screen reader narration, and plain language everywhere.

## Under the hood (the short version)
A multi-agent retrieval pipeline blends hybrid semantic search with exact structured queries over NYC Open Data, including 311 service requests, NYPD crash data, and neighborhood boundaries. It sits on a 3D OpenStreetMap city, with voice in and voice out.

## Demo win conditions
- Walk 5 blocks hands-free, with narration for each block in under 2 seconds.
- 5 spoken questions, each with a correct number checked against city data.
- An Accountability report for any neighborhood in under 10 seconds.
- A screen reader user finishes the full walk without a mouse.

## Honest by design
It shows what people reported and how the city responded. It doesn't predict crime or score people, and crash data runs about four months behind. We say that out loud, on screen.
